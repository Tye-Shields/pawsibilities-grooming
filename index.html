About

Nicole is your person

Pawsibilities Grooming is a dog grooming salon on Main Street East in Hamilton. Customers say Nicole is detail-oriented and patient, cares a great deal about each dog's experience, gives tips for maintenance at home, and keeps a clean, organised salon. Some drive in from out of town.
